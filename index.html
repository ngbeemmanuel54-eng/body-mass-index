What your BMI result means

BMI is one tool used to understand weight in relation to height. However, it does not measure every aspect of health. Age, muscle mass and other factors matter too.

♡

Healthy eating

Eating a variety of nutritious foods supports health, energy and general well-being.

↗

Regular exercise

Regular physical activity supports fitness, strength and overall health.

☾

Adequate sleep

Good sleep supports concentration, mood and recovery.

Limitations of BMI

BMI is not a complete measure of health. Results should be interpreted carefully because body composition and individual circumstances differ.

⚥ Gender and age

Age and development affect how BMI should be interpreted.

♧ Age

Changes in body composition can affect BMI interpretation.

♧ Muscle

Muscle mass can influence BMI without indicating excess fat.

◉ Pregnancy

Pregnancy changes weight, so standard BMI interpretation differs.

✿ Individual differences

Many factors beyond BMI contribute to overall health.

BMI is an estimate and not a medical diagnosis.
